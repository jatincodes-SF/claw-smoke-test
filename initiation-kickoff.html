1. Engagement Kickoff & Objectives

Team, we are officially kicking off the Initiation Phase for our new client engagement. The objective of this phase is to align on scope boundaries, governance, architectural foundations, technical feasibility, and team readiness before proceeding into active sprint execution.

Critical Open Questions for Tomorrow's Stakeholder Sync

  • Environment & Infrastructure Access: Do we have dedicated client cloud sandbox environments and VPN/SSO credentials ready, or what is the lead time for provisioning?
  • Client Decision-Makers & Sign-off Velocity: Who holds final approval for scope trade-offs and architectural sign-offs, and what is the expected SLA for sign-off turnaround?
  • Third-Party Dependencies & Integrations: Are third-party vendor APIs, sandboxes, and documentation fully accessible and rate-limit cleared for development?
  • Target Timelines & Budget Milestones: Are there fixed hard deadlines (e.g. board demos, compliance audit dates, conference releases) that dictate scope prioritization?
  • Data Compliance & Security Protocols: Are there specific compliance regimes (e.g., HIPAA, SOC2, GDPR) requiring client-mandated code scanners, data masking, or audit trails?

3. Immediate Action Items & Next Steps

  1. Team Sync: Final review of open questions and roles before client call (15 mins prior to stakeholder sync).
  2. Stakeholder Call Execution: Lead the discussion around Charter, RAID log, and Access timelines.
  3. Action Item Dispatch: Share formal meeting minutes, updated RAID log, and finalized environment access matrix by EOD tomorrow.
